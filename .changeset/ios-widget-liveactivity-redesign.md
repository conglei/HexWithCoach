Redesign the iOS Home Screen widget and dictation surfaces. The placeholder widget is replaced with a one-tap "Start dictation" widget (small + medium) that shows a live "Keyboard on / Enable keyboard" status and, on the medium size, a review-streak glance; tapping it either starts a session or jumps straight to Settings to finish enabling the keyboard. The Lock Screen Live Activity is simplified to a single calm "Ready to dictate" card (no jargon, no app name), with the live "speaking now" waveform kept to the Dynamic Island where it's actually seen. Adds a Control Center / Lock Screen "Start dictation" button so you can begin dictating into another app in one tap.
