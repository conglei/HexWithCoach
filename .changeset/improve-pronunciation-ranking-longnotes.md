Coach: "Sounds to work on" now ranks by how actionable a sound is, not by raw frequency. Long notes were dominated by the most common reduced sounds (schwa, /t/, /ɹ/) — the least teachable — because lessons were sorted by instance count. The list now prioritizes severity and clear substitutions, and caps reduced vowels to at most one slot, so the most fixable sounds surface first (#84).
