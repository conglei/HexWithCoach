Coach UX: pronunciation feedback is now summary-first — a "Sounds to work on" card surfaces your 1–3 weakest sounds with the substitution you made ("expected /ɛ/ → you said /ə/") and your own example words, instead of an overwhelming per-phoneme dump. The phoneme model now decodes the actually-pronounced sound (`actualSymbol`) so the per-word drill-down leads with the worst sound and its expected→actual contrast. The inline COACH panel now shows all word-choice/grammar issues with a "1 of N" browser, and tapping an underline jumps the panel to that issue.
